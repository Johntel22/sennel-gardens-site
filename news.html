Governor Moore Announces Nearly $4 Million in NourishMD Grants to Expand Fresh Food Access in Food Deserts

July 27, 2026 • Office of the Governor of Maryland

Sennel Gardens was named one of 44 organizations statewide to receive a share of nearly $4 million from the Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development's NourishMD Grant Program, expanding fresh food access in Maryland's food deserts.
